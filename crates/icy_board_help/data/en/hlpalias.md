# Help: (ALIAS) name change

This command allows you to turn your alias on or off in conferences
which permit alias usage.

## Subcommands

- `ON` Use your alias. Your alias will be active if in a conference
  where aliases are allowed.
- `OFF` Use your real name. Even though you are in a conference where
  aliases are allowed, your real name will be used.

## Description

When you are in a conference that allows aliases, this command enables
you to decide when your alias will be active.
If your alias is active and you execute this command with no
subcommands, you will switch to using your real name.

This command has no effect in conferences where aliases are forbidden.

## Examples

This example will show you what to enter if you want to toggle the
status of your alias from either on to off or from off to on:

```text
ALIAS
```

To turn usage of your alias on, enter:

```text
ALIAS ON
```
